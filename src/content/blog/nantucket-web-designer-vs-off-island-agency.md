**Short answer:** an off-island agency gives you a bigger team and a more corporate process, at a higher price and with no real understanding of the island. A Nantucket designer gives you someone who knows the difference between Mid-Island and downtown foot traffic, can shoot your photos in person, and answers the phone in the off-season. For most island small businesses, the local designer is the better fit. For a few, the agency is. Here is how to tell.

I should be clear about my bias. I was born on Nantucket, I build websites for island businesses, and I currently split time between the island and Boston. I am also honest enough to tell you when an agency is the right call.

## What local knowledge actually buys

**Photography that is real.** Visitors know what Brant Point, Main Street, and the Madaket sunset look like. A site built with stock photos of some other beach reads as fake instantly. A local designer shoots your actual place. An off-island agency licenses stock or asks you to send phone photos. More on this in [website photography vs stock](/blog/website-photography-vs-stock-photos-nantucket).

**Seasonal sense.** A local designer knows to build in the off-season, launch before Daffodil, and never schedule a review call during Stroll weekend. They know your hours change five times a year and build for it.

**Geography and vocabulary.** Knowing that a caretaking client's customers are searching from Boston in February, that charter guests do not know Straight Wharf from Old South Wharf, that "ACK" means something. This shapes what pages exist and what they say. See the [local SEO playbook](/blog/local-seo-nantucket-businesses) for how this vocabulary turns into rankings.

**Accountability.** On an island of roughly 15,000 year-round residents, reputation is everything. A local designer who does bad work hears about it at the Stop and Shop. That is a stronger guarantee than any contract.

**Relationships.** A local designer knows the Chamber, the Current, the Inquirer and Mirror, and the other businesses you should be linked with. That network is part of what you are hiring.

## Where agencies win

**Scale.** If you are building something with hundreds of pages, multiple languages, or a custom software platform, a team beats a solo designer.

**Specialized departments.** Some projects need a dedicated copywriter, a paid ads manager, and a developer all at once. Agencies have them on staff.

**Process and paperwork.** Larger organizations and nonprofits with boards sometimes need a formal proposal, a project manager, and weekly status reports. Agencies are built for that.

If your project needs those things, hire an agency. Most island businesses need a fast, beautiful five to ten page site with real photos, and an agency's overhead is paying for departments you will never use.

## Cost comparison

Agency pricing for a small business site in Massachusetts commonly starts around $4,000 and climbs fast, with monthly retainers on top. Templated "Nantucket web design" pages from off-island shops sometimes advertise low monthly fees that never end.

A local designer's starter site runs from about two thousand dollars, and you own it. The numbers are laid out in [how much a website costs on Nantucket](/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost-nantucket).

## How to check who is really on-island

Some websites ranking for "Nantucket web design" are the same page with the town name swapped, published for a dozen towns. Here is how to spot them.

- Search the company name with another town. If "Kennebunk" or "Milford" shows up with the identical page, they are not here.
- Look for real Nantucket client sites you can click through to.
- Ask where they will be in February and whether they can shoot photos at your location.
- Check whether the page mentions anything specific about the island beyond the name. Ferry lines, neighborhoods, events, actual client names.

## Questions to ask either way

Whichever direction you go, the questions in [12 questions to ask before hiring a web designer](/blog/questions-to-ask-before-hiring-a-web-designer-nantucket) apply. Ownership, process, cost, and results matter more than the address.

## My recommendation

Small business, island customers, real photos, real budget? Hire local. Enterprise scale, formal process, multiple departments? Hire an agency and make sure they have at least one person who has been to the island.
